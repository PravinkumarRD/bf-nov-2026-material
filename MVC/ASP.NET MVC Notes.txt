What is MVC?
Model-View-Controller

Why MVC?

1) Handles Separation of Concern
2) Easy maintenance 
3) Supports TDD - Test Driven Development
4) Reusability 
5) Loose Coupling
6) Develop Scalable application
7) Easy Testing
8) SEO Friendly applications

MVC Frameworks - 
1) Routing [Human Readable/Testable]
2) IoC and D.I.
3) Authentication and Authorization 
4) State Management

	https://localhost:2672/Employees [Named/Specific Route] [/Employees/Index]
	https://localhost:2672/Employees/List [Named/Specific Route] [/Employees/List]
	https://localhost:2672/Employees/Details/782 [Dynamic/Parameterized Route] [/Employees/Details/782]


Controller - the destination where your request will be processed [Ultimate Destination - Action Method].

1) Controller can work alone
2) Controller can work only with model
3) Controller can work only with view
4) Controller can work with model and view
